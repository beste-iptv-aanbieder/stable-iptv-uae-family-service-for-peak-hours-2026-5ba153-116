Anti‑Throttling Protection

Your ISP (Internet Service Provider) may slow down streaming traffic during peak hours. This is called throttling. SubTV IP recommends you use Cloudflare DNS (1.1.1.1) and set your buffer or cache to 5,000 ms. These steps help avoid slowdowns and keep your stream stable.

According to networking experts at Harvard University, using a public DNS like 1.1.1.1 can improve connection reliability for streaming. This is a simple change you can make in your router or device settings.

Step‑by‑Step Setup for Peak‑Hour Stability

Follow these steps to get the best performance from SubTV IP:

  1. Change your DNS: Set your primary DNS to 1.1.1.1 (Cloudflare) on your router or on each streaming device.
  2. Increase buffer size: In your player app (e.g., TiviMate), set the buffer / cache to 5,000 ms. This gives the app time to pre‑load video.
  3. Use a wired connection if possible: Ethernet is more stable than Wi‑Fi, especially during peak hours.
  4. Restart your router weekly: This clears temporary caches and keeps your connection fresh.
